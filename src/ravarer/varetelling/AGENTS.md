- New stock-count Edge functions live as source in `supabase/pending/functions/` until approved; their pure cores (`ocrCore.ts`, `reportCore.ts`) are imported by the synthetic client and vitest. Why: files in `supabase/functions/` may be auto-deployed before review.
- Paper count-sheet reading stores proposals only; counted values are written solely via `vt_save_line` after user confirmation, and document identity is proven against the print manifest, never by the model echoing expected IDs. Why: AI output is not document evidence.
- Report values, CSV and PDF content for screen, download and email come only from `supabase/pending/functions/_shared/vtReport.ts` (exact decimal half-up per line). Why: separate implementations diverged on states, rounding and page bounds.
- Email send ops stay in the browser per user/company/count until a known terminal state or explicit user reconciliation; reservation is service-only with the JWT-verified actor and Edge-built snapshot/attachments, and resume requires the original actor (or approver) plus the identical stored request. Why: client-callable reserve allowed fabricated attachments, and removing the op on any reply allowed duplicate sends.
- Valuation = frozen snapshot price, else the active per-count clarification (`vt_price_clarifications`); snapshots and global prices are never changed. Why: many materials lack documented price, and finishing must not require editing global cost data.
- Paper reading validates against the issued `vt_print_manifests` row, never a reconstruction. Why: historic sheets must match what was actually printed.
- Every report surface (screen totals, archive, printed report, CSV/PDF, email gate) reads lines only through `useClarifiedLines`, and output is blocked unless its `status` is `ready`. Why: the printed report read raw lines and ignored approved prices.
- OCR batches: every claim/continue issues a new `attempt_id` (ownership token). Auto-continue only after the previous batch is durably released; an in-flight batch past its deadline becomes `ukjent_ai_utfall` (stored pages kept) and needs approver confirmation. If all pages are stored, finalize from them without re-reading. Why: an expired lease could still have a running paid call, and a late write could overwrite a newer batch.
- Original viewing uses the server-stored `page_sources`/proposal `source` (file + PDF page + preserved original), never the printed page number as an index. Why: reordered uploads, multi-page PDFs and WEBP pairs showed the wrong page.
